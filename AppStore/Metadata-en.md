## Description

One finger. Endless space.

In Orbit Rush, you circle glowing planets and choose the perfect moment to launch. Steer through space, collect stars, destroy asteroids and correct a doomed flight before it is too late.

• Fast sessions with simple one-finger controls
• Mid-flight steering and risky rescue maneuvers
• Precision shooting with swipe gestures
• Slow motion, star magnet and triple shot power-ups
• Boss planets, comets and moving targets
• Daily Run, training mode and personal ghost runs
• Player levels, XP and persistent statistics
• Custom photos as planet textures
• Worldwide leaderboards through Apple Game Center
• Premium nearby and worldwide multiplayer
• No ads and no tracking

How far can you go?

## App Review Notes

Solo play requires no account. Optional paid multiplayer uses Apple Game Center for worldwide matches and the local network for nearby matches. The optional photo picker uses Apple's system PhotosPicker; selected images remain local and are not uploaded. Non-consumable in-app purchases are available in the Orbit Shop and can be restored.
